# CareBridge AI: Problem Discovery

## Product Concept

**CareBridge AI** is a product concept focused on helping recently discharged patients better understand and manage clinician-approved post-discharge instructions.

The initial concept is not to replace clinical judgment. The product would help patients interpret, organize, and follow information already provided by their care team.

## Problem Statement

Patients often leave a healthcare setting with multi-step discharge or post-operative instructions that may be lengthy, difficult to understand, or easy to forget.

Some patients may begin reading the instructions but stop when the information feels complex or overwhelming. Others may rely primarily on verbal guidance received during discharge and may later struggle to remember what they were told.

This creates a potential gap between receiving instructions and successfully understanding or acting on them.

## Primary User Hypothesis

The initial target user is:

> **A recently discharged adult patient who receives multi-step post-discharge instructions and is responsible for managing their own follow-up care.**

This is a working hypothesis and would need validation through user research.

## Current Experience

A likely current-state journey may look like this:

Patient receives printed or digital discharge instructions → begins reviewing them → encounters complex or unclear information → relies on memory or verbal guidance → may delay, misunderstand, or overlook a follow-up task.

This journey is a hypothesis based on the problem space and should be validated with patients and clinical stakeholders.

## Initial User Needs

Potential user needs include:

- Understand what they need to do next
- Know which instructions are most important
- Receive information in simpler language
- Get reminders for time-sensitive follow-up tasks
- Ask questions about approved instructions
- Know when to contact a clinician or care team member

## AI Opportunity Hypothesis

AI may create value when the user needs help understanding or interacting with complex instructions.

Potential AI-assisted capabilities include:

- Simplifying clinician-approved instructions
- Organizing instructions into clear next steps
- Translating approved content
- Answering questions grounded in authorized source material
- Identifying when a question requires escalation to a human

The product should not use AI simply because AI is available. Each capability should be evaluated against conventional software alternatives for reliability, cost, safety, and user value.

## Initial Experience Concept

One possible experience is a QR code on discharge materials that directs the patient to a simple digital care plan.

The experience could organize tasks into categories such as:

**Today**  
**Tomorrow**  
**Follow-Up**

The patient could then interact with options such as:

- “I understand”
- “Explain this in simpler language”
- “Remind me”
- “Contact my care team”

This is an early concept, not a validated solution.

## Product Boundaries

CareBridge AI should not independently:

- Diagnose a condition
- Prescribe treatment
- Change medication dosage or frequency
- Modify clinician-approved instructions
- Alter a care plan
- Make unsupported clinical recommendations

If the system cannot answer safely or lacks sufficient grounding, the experience should escalate to an approved human contact.

## Early Success Measures

Potential measures include:

- QR activation rate
- Instruction completion or acknowledgement rate
- Patient comprehension
- Reminder engagement
- Escalation rate
- Percentage of AI responses that meet accuracy, groundedness, completeness, clarity, and safety criteria

Activation and engagement metrics alone should not be treated as proof that the underlying patient problem has been solved.

## Key Assumptions to Validate

Before moving toward solution design, the team would need to validate whether patients:

- Actually struggle to understand discharge instructions
- Prefer digital support after discharge
- Would use a QR-based care experience
- Benefit from simplified explanations
- Trust AI-assisted explanations when clearly constrained
- Need reminders or interactive Q&A
- Prefer human escalation for certain questions

## Product Discovery Principle

The guiding question for this concept is:

> **What patient problem are we solving, and is AI the best mechanism for solving it?**

CareBridge AI should move forward only where AI creates meaningful user value without introducing unacceptable clinical or operational risk.
