# ProductTrace — Market Intelligence Research Brief

## Decision to support

How should ProductTrace differentiate its evidence-to-decision and Market Intelligence workflow from current AI-assisted product discovery and customer intelligence platforms?

## Market

AI-assisted product discovery, product management, and customer intelligence platforms.

## Target customer

Product Managers, Product Operations, and product leaders in B2B SaaS and enterprise environments.

## Initial competitor set

- Productboard
- Dovetail
- Jira Product Discovery

## Geography

Global / English-language SaaS market.

## Timeframe

Current-state research captured September 22, 2026.

## Research questions

1. How do competitors capture and organize product/customer evidence?
2. How is AI used to synthesize insights, themes, or ideas?
3. How do competitors connect evidence to prioritization or product decisions?
4. What pricing/plan patterns are visible?
5. What source-traceability or governance capabilities are explicitly documented?
6. Where could ProductTrace differentiate without making unsupported claims?

## Scope

This is a bounded official-source audit, not a full market-size, buyer-research, or win/loss study.

## Product principle

> Every market claim used by ProductTrace should remain traceable to a captured source.
